Privacy Policy

Investment AI OS Backup is a private application operated by its owner for backing up a PostgreSQL database to the owner's Google Drive account.

The application uses Google Drive access solely to upload, verify, retain and retrieve encrypted database backup files.

Database backup files are encrypted before upload. The private decryption key is not stored in Google Drive or on the backup server.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Access to Google Drive can be revoked by the account owner in Google Account security settings.

This application is not offered to the public.
